The short version

  • We collect only the information you choose to send us, plus the minimal technical data any website host receives.
  • We do not sell personal information, and this site does not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.
  • If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal, the site’s optional usage measurement is switched off entirely.

Information you provide

If you use the contact form, we receive the details you enter: your name, phone number and/or email address, the type of matter and your message. We use this information to respond to you and to check for conflicts of interest before agreeing to represent anyone.

Please do not send confidential or time-sensitive information through this website. Information you send before we have agreed in writing to represent you may not be treated as confidential or privileged.

Information collected automatically

Hosting

Like all websites, our hosting provider receives technical information when you visit — such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested — and may keep it in server logs for security and reliability purposes.

Optional usage measurement

Where enabled, the site records anonymous, aggregate events — for example, which sections of a page were viewed, whether a phone or directions link was used, and basic page-performance measurements. These events never include the contents of forms, are filtered to remove anything resembling an email address or phone number, and are not used to identify you. We do not use third-party advertising or analytics scripts.

Browser storage

The site uses your browser’s session storage to remember, for the current visit only, that you have already seen the opening animation and to prevent duplicate form submissions. It does not set cookies.

How information is shared

We share information only with service providers who help us operate the website or receive inquiries (for example, our hosting provider), when required by law or professional rules, or with your permission. We do not sell or rent personal information.

Retention and security

We keep inquiry information as long as reasonably necessary to respond, to maintain conflict-of-interest records and to meet our professional recordkeeping obligations. We use reasonable safeguards to protect information, but no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.
