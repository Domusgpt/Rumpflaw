Questions

Questions we hear often.

Don’t see yours? Call the office at (609) 294-2227.

How do I schedule a consultation?

Call the office at (609) 294-2227 during business hours. We’ll ask a few questions about your matter, check for conflicts of interest and set a time to meet.

What should I bring to a first meeting?

Bring any documents related to your matter — contracts, notices, court papers, prior wills, insurance information — along with a list of your questions. If you’re not sure whether something is relevant, bring it.

How are fees handled?

It depends on the matter. Some matters are handled for a flat fee, others hourly, and certain injury claims on a contingent-fee basis as New Jersey court rules permit. We explain the arrangement in writing before we begin.

Does contacting the firm make me a client?

No. An attorney-client relationship begins only after we have confirmed there is no conflict of interest and agreed in writing to represent you. Until then, please share only general information about your matter.

I’ve been named executor of an estate. What happens next?

Generally, the will is probated with the Surrogate in the county where the person lived — here, the Ocean County Surrogate. From there, the executor gathers assets, gives required notices, addresses any New Jersey inheritance tax and debts, and makes distributions. We can guide you through each step.

Do you only handle matters in Ocean County?

Our practice is centered in southern Ocean County, but we may be able to help with matters elsewhere in New Jersey. Call and ask — if we can’t help, we’ll try to point you in the right direction.
